# Shops and lists

!!! note "Planned"
    This arrives in Phases 2–3 of the [work plan](../WORKPLAN.md).

## Shops
A **shop** is a place you buy things: "Rimi", "Hardware store", "Pharmacy". Each person has
their own shops; reorder them by dragging. Each shop shows how many items are still open.

Every shop has one or more **lists** ("Weekly", "Party on Saturday"). One of them is the
**default list**, marked with a star: it's where new items go when you don't pick a list, for
example from quick-add in the shop switcher, from [Mealie](mealie.md) or from the [API](../api.md).
Make another list the default from its menu.

## Categories
A list is split into **categories** such as Dairy, Meat, Vegetables. Order them like the
aisles of the shop by dragging them; collapse the ones you don't need right now. Items without
a category are under *Other* at the end. A new list in a shop copies the categories of the
shop's default list, so you only arrange the aisles once.

## Adding items
Type in the box at the top and press Enter. An amount at the end is recognised: `milk 2`,
`flour 1 kg`.

While you type, **suggestions from everything you've added before** appear right away; tap one
to add it. It goes straight into its usual category with its usual amount. With the box empty,
your most frequent items for this shop that aren't on the list yet are shown as chips: one tap
each.

Adding something that's already on the list adds to its amount instead of making a second
row.

## Changing amounts
Every item has **−** and **+** buttons. The step fits the unit: 1 for pieces and packs, 0.5 for
kg and litres, 100 for grams and millilitres. Tap the amount itself to type an exact value or
change the unit. Pressing − at the lowest amount removes the item (with undo).

## Your item catalogue
Everything you've added is remembered in your personal **item catalogue**. In
**Settings → Item catalogue** you can rename entries, set their usual category, unit and step,
merge duplicates (say *Tomato* and *Tomatoes*) and hide or delete what you no longer buy.

## Shopping
Tap an item to put it **in the cart**; it moves to the bottom so the rest of the list stays
short. Tap again to take it out. When you're done, **Finish shopping** clears the cart (with
undo for a few seconds).

If the signal in the shop is bad, keep ticking: changes are saved on the phone and sent as
soon as the connection is back. A banner shows how many are waiting.

## Staples
**Staples** are what you usually buy at a shop. Open *Add staples* on a list to see them as a
checklist; the ones not already on the list are pre-selected. Turn any item into a staple
from its menu.

## Brands and pictures
Items can be a specific brand with a picture ("milk by Alma"); see
[Brands and pictures](products.md).

## Ordering and moving
Drag items within a category or into another category; on a phone, press and hold to lift.
With a keyboard: focus an item, press ++space++ to pick it up, the arrow keys to move it and
++space++ to drop it. To move items to another list or shop, select them and choose *Move to…*.

## Sharing
Lists are private until you share them; see [Households and sharing](sharing.md).
