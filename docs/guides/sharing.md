# Households and sharing

## Private by default
Everything you create (shops, lists, wishlists, gift lists) is **yours and private**. Nobody
else sees it, not even people in your household, until you share it.

## Households
A **household** is a group of people you often share with ("Home", "Parents"). You can be in
several. Anyone can create one in **Settings → Households** and becomes its **owner**. Owners
rename it, add people, make others owners and remove members; anyone can leave. A household
always keeps at least one owner: if the last one leaves, the longest-standing member takes over.
Admins can manage every household.

## Sharing a list
Open a list's **⋯** menu → **Share…**. Share it with:

- a **household** you're in: everyone in it, including people who join later, or
- individual **people**.

Each share is either **editor** (add, tick, edit and reorder items and categories) or
**viewer** (read only). Only you, the owner, can rename, delete or re-share the list.

The share sheet shows who can see the list and why (a household, with who's in it now, or
invited directly). Removing a share, or removing someone from the household, takes effect
immediately, also on phones that have the list open.

Lists shared with you show up under **Shared with me** on the Shopping page, with the owner's
shop and name. What you add there goes into *your* item catalogue. The owner's shop and templates
stay theirs. In the list's **⋯** menu → **Who can see it…** you can leave a list that was shared
with you personally; for a list shared with a household, leave the household instead.

## Live updates and bad signal
Changes appear on everyone's screens within about a second. If the connection drops in the
shop, keep going: ticks, amounts and new items are saved on the phone, a banner says how many
are waiting, and they are sent in order as soon as the connection is back. Then the app
catches up with what others changed meanwhile. Creating shops and lists and editing the
catalogue need a connection.

Wishlists and gift lists are shared the same way; for gift lists the recipient is always
excluded (see [Wishlists and gifts](wishlists.md)).
