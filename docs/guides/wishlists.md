# Wishlists and gifts

!!! note "Planned"
    This arrives in Phase 5 of the [work plan](../WORKPLAN.md).

The **Wishlist** tab has two parts: **My wishlists** and **Gift ideas**.

## My wishlists
Things you'd like yourself. Split a wishlist into **topic sections** ("Books", "Kitchen",
"Hiking") and give items a link, a price, a note and a priority. Drag to reorder items and
sections.

Your wishlists are private until you share them, typically with your household so family
knows what you'd like (see [Households and sharing](sharing.md)).

**You never see who claimed what on your own wishlist**, so surprises stay surprises. When you
get something, mark it **received** to move it to the archive.

## Gift ideas
Gift lists are about **someone else**: a household member, or anyone by name ("Grandma").
Collect ideas for them throughout the year, with sections like your own wishlist.

!!! important "The recipient never sees it"
    A gift list for a person who has an account here is hidden from that person everywhere:
    in the app, in counts, in live updates, in exports and in the API, even if the list is
    shared with a household they belong to.

## Claims
On a wishlist or gift list shared with you, tap **I'll get this** on an item so nobody else
buys it too. Everyone who can see the list (except the person it's for) sees that it's taken
and by whom. Change it to **bought** once you have it, or release it if you change your mind.
