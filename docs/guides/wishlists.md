# Wishlists and gifts

The **Wishlist** tab has **My wishlists**, **From others** (wishlists people shared with you)
and **Gift ideas**.

## My wishlists
Things you'd like yourself. **Add** items with a link, a price, a note and how much you want
them (★ would like a lot, ★★ most wanted). Split a wishlist into **topic sections** ("Books",
"Kitchen", "Hiking") in its **⋯** menu, and drag items and sections into order. A link shows
its site's name; nothing is fetched from it. Prices use your currency from
**Settings → Profile** (EUR unless you change it).

Your wishlists are private until you share them, typically with your household so family
knows what you'd like (see [Households and sharing](sharing.md)).

**You never see who claimed what on your own wishlist**, so surprises stay surprises. When you
get something, mark it **Received** in its menu to move it to the archive at the bottom (and
back, if needed). An item can also be copied to another wishlist, e.g. a gift idea into your
own.

## Gift ideas
Gift lists are about **someone else**: a household member, or anyone by name ("Grandma").
Choose **New gift list for… → Someone new**, give their name and, if they have an account
here, link it. Collect ideas for them throughout the year, with sections like your own
wishlist, and share the list with the others who give them presents.

!!! important "The recipient never sees it"
    A gift list for a person linked to an account here is hidden from that person everywhere:
    in the app, in counts, in live updates and in the API, even if the list is shared with a
    household they belong to. They get no hint that it exists. If you link the account later,
    it disappears for them at that moment. You can't share a gift list with the person it's
    for, nor make one about yourself.

## Claims
On a wishlist or gift list shared with you, tap **I'll get this** on an item so nobody else
buys it too. Everyone who can see the list (except the person it's for) sees that it's taken
and by whom. Change it to **bought** once you have it, or release it if you change your mind.
